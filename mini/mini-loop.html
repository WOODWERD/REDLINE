Guide

Start a review

Take a screen grab (on a Mac, ⌘⇧4 then hold Control as you drag puts it on the clipboard; on Windows, Win+Shift+S), then press Ctrl+V or ⌘V on this page. You can also drop a PNG or JPG, or ask Claude in a chat to “mini loop this”.

Paste while an image is open and the grab becomes its next version (v1, v2…), so a fix in Fusion is one grab away from the next round.

Your PDF stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and your markups are saved in this browser until you clear its data.

Mark up

VSelect, move, Delete removes HPan (or hold Space) CCloud an area RBox AArrow TCallout with note text PPen IHighlight MMeasure at the chosen scale WFit width · Ctrl 0 fit page

Cite a standard

Under any markup, press Cite a standard and type a word (aisle, landing, toilet, revision) to tag it with an NCS drafting convention, an NKBA kitchen or bath guideline, or an ICON 3D-printed wall rule. Tag a Measure and it is checked against the numbers: ✓ meets, ! meets the minimum but not the recommendation, ✗ fails. Standards at the top lists them all.

Screen grabs have no scale until you set one. Press Set scale, drag along something whose length you know, and type it (36", 3'-0" or 914mm). Measures then read in feet and inches and tagged ones are checked. Perspective views do not measure true; use an orthographic view.

Send it back

Type your name and press Download marked-up PNG. Send the file back however you like: email, text, a shared folder. Each markup is a real PDF comment, so it shows in Bluebeam, Acrobat, Preview or any PDF viewer.

Send for review

Markups stay drafts until you press Submit edits. Claude is called right away and posts a response round (for example X1.0.17r): a reply under every markup with the planned change or a question. No drawing happens yet.

Answer Claude

Under each reply, press Approve plan (or Accept and close when it's done), or Answer / change to send a rework note. When you have been through them, press Commit changes. Claude is called again, draws the approved changes and issues the next version (X1.0.18). Rework notes get a fresh reply first if they change the plan. Accepted, finished markups close and drop out of the open count. Needs my review in the filter shows what's waiting on you.

Send a PDF to someone

Download marked-up PNG saves the image with your markups drawn on it.

How it works

Three steps, no account needed.
  1. Open the PDF. Pick Cloud, Arrow or Callout and drag on the drawing.
  2. Type what should change in the comment box beside each markup.
  3. Type your name, press Download marked-up PNG, and send the file back however you like.

Work with Claude? WOODWERD Loop runs this same page as a review cycle: Claude works your markups and sends back the next revision.

02

Start from a chat

The quickest way to loop on a render Claude makes.
  1. In a chat, ask Claude for a render or capture and say “mini loop this”, or attach your own image. Or paste a screen grab straight onto this page.
  2. Claude adds it here and replies with a link straight to it.
  3. Mark it up and press Submit edits. Claude replies under each markup, then you approve or answer and press Commit changes for the next version.
